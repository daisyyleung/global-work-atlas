# Sample-data provenance

All bundled sample tasks, teams, workstreams, descriptions, dates, counts, and activity are fictional and newly authored for this repository. They must not paraphrase or encode private records.

Sample state must declare its fictional status in machine-readable metadata and the interface. Use generic invented labels, avoid people and email addresses, and keep examples clearly educational. Tests and privacy scanning must fail if the fictional marker is absent.
